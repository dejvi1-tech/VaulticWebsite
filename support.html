Frequently Asked Questions

🔑 I forgot my PIN. Can I get back in?

If you set up a recovery key before forgetting your PIN, yes. On the lock screen tap "Forgot PIN?", enter your 24-word phrase, and you'll be asked to create a new PIN immediately.

If you did not set up a recovery key, there is no recovery path — this is intentional. The vault is designed so that only the PIN holder can decrypt it. In this case you'll need to delete and reinstall the app to start fresh.

📝 I set up a recovery key. Where is it stored?

Nowhere by us. The 24-word phrase is shown to you exactly once — you must write it down and keep it safe. A copy of the master key wrapped by the phrase is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only. We never transmit, log, or see the phrase.

🗑️ What happens if I uninstall the app?

All encrypted files and metadata are deleted with the app. The iOS Keychain entries (wrapped master key, biometric secret) survive uninstall by default on iOS — this is iOS behaviour, not specific to Vaultic. To fully clear them, reinstall the app and use the Erase Vault option in Settings, which explicitly deletes the Keychain entries.

☁️ Is my vault backed up to iCloud?

By default, no. Vault files are stored in Application Support, which iCloud does not back up automatically, and the app does not sync your vault to iCloud on its own.

With Vaultic Pro, you can enable optional encrypted iCloud Drive backup. The backup is AES-256-GCM encrypted on your device before upload — Apple only ever receives opaque ciphertext. The decryption key never leaves your device.

🌐 Does Vaultic use the internet?

Yes. There is no Vaultic server: we operate no backend that can receive, store, or read your vault, there is no account, and there is no analytics or telemetry SDK of our own. Four features reach the network, each only when you choose to use it:

  • Private Browser — loads the pages you visit, directly from those sites.
  • Encrypted iCloud Backup (optional, Pro) — uploads a sealed archive to your own iCloud.
  • Device Transfer (optional, Pro) — sends an encrypted copy to a device you pair with, over your local network only.
  • Purchases — handled by Apple's App Store.

The free version may also load ads from Google AdMob — see "Does Vaultic show ads?" below.

Apart from ads, if you never open the Private Browser, never enable iCloud Backup, never run a transfer, and never buy Pro, Vaultic doesn't use the network. You can confirm that with a proxy such as Charles or mitmproxy.

Two mechanical checks back this up: a pre-build script fails the build if a general-purpose networking API or any known analytics SDK appears in the source, and it confines each path above — advertising included — to the one part of the code that owns it.

📢 Does Vaultic show ads?

The free version may show ads provided by Google AdMob. A Vaultic Pro subscription (monthly or yearly) or the one-time Lifetime purchase removes them.

Ads never get access to your vault. Your photos, videos, documents, album names, PIN, recovery key, and Private Browser URLs are not sent to Google for advertising. Google may process some device, network, and advertising-related information to show ads — see Advertising in our Privacy Policy.

Ads may be personalised where you allow it, and otherwise Google serves another eligible ad mode. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland — and for state privacy choices in the United States — Google's own consent message asks first, and Settings → Privacy Choices reopens it. On iOS the free version also shows Apple's tracking permission prompt; if you decline, ads still work, just without your device's advertising identifier. Pro and Lifetime never load an ad, so they never see either prompt.

🔒 Where do my encrypted files actually live on disk?

Inside the iOS Application Support container:

  • Library/Application Support/vault-real/blobs/ — encrypted file blobs
  • Library/Application Support/vault-real/vault.sqlite — encrypted metadata

Every file has NSFileProtectionComplete set, meaning the OS hardware encryption key is unavailable while the device is locked.

🕵️ What is the Private Browser?

A browser inside Vaultic that runs on a non-persistent session: cookies, cache, and history from a browsing session aren't kept by Vaultic once the session ends, and a built-in rule list blocks common third-party trackers.

To be clear about what it is not: it reduces the browsing traces kept inside Vaultic. It does not make you anonymous. The websites you visit, your internet provider, your network operator, and any VPN you use can still see your traffic in the normal way. It is a privacy convenience, not an anonymity tool.

💳 How do I manage or cancel my Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone to view, change, or cancel your Vaultic Pro subscription. We have no access to your subscription or payment details.

🔄 I cancelled Pro. Can I still access my backed-up files?

Yes — always. Restore is free forever, even after cancelling Pro. You can download and decrypt any backup you created while subscribed at any time.

📲 How does Device Transfer work?

Device Transfer moves your entire vault directly to another iPhone over your local Wi-Fi network. No cloud, no cable, no account needed. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network, and both confirm a one-time pairing code before any data moves. The transfer is end-to-end encrypted — the vault data is encrypted before it leaves the source device and remains encrypted in transit.
