Security Model

Open, verifiable, uncompromising.

We don't ask you to trust us. Here's exactly how it works.

  • AES-GCM 256-bit encryption for every file. Unique nonce per blob. Authentication tag failure = wrong PIN.
  • PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 200,000 iterations to derive the wrapping key from your PIN.
  • Master key never stored in cleartext. Wrapped in the iOS Keychain under kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly.
  • 10-attempt wipe. Ten consecutive wrong PINs permanently delete the master key. No recovery without your phrase.
  • Privacy overlay covers the UI in the App Switcher. What you have stays private.
  • Auto-lock zeroes the in-memory master key when the app backgrounds. Configurable delay.
# On-disk format for every encrypted blob
nonce[12] || ciphertext || tag[16]

# Master key derivation
salt ← SecRandomCopyBytes(16)
wrapping_key ← PBKDF2(PIN, salt, 200_000)
wrapped ← AES-GCM.seal(master_key, wrapping_key)

# Keychain storage
kSecAttrAccessible:
  WhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly
kSecAttrSynchronizable: false
Network

When Vaultic uses the internet.

Vaultic is not an offline-only app, and we won't pretend otherwise. Here is every network path, and what it carries.

  • No Vaultic server. We operate no backend that receives, stores, or processes your vault. There is no account. We cannot read your files.
  • No analytics or telemetry of our own. No analytics SDK, no attribution framework, no remote configuration. The free version's ad SDK is Google's, and we switch its crash reporting off.
  • Private Browser loads the pages you visit, directly from those sites — like any browser.
  • Encrypted iCloud Backup (optional, Pro) uploads a passphrase-sealed archive to your iCloud. Apple holds ciphertext; the key stays on your device.
  • Device Transfer (optional, Pro) sends an encrypted copy to a device you pair with, over your local network only.
  • Purchases go through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Ads (free version only) may be loaded from Google AdMob, with Google's consent SDK alongside it. Ads may be personalised where you allow it, which on iOS means the free version asks for Apple's tracking permission — declining costs nothing but the advertising identifier. Your vault contents are never sent to Google for advertising. Vaultic Pro and Lifetime remove ads entirely.
  • Nothing else. Apart from ads in the free version, if you never open the Private Browser, never enable iCloud Backup, never run a transfer, and never buy Pro, Vaultic doesn't use the network.
