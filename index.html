Security Model

Open, verifiable, uncompromising.

We don't ask you to trust us. Here's exactly how it works.

  • AES-GCM 256-bit encryption for every file. Unique nonce per blob. Authentication tag failure = wrong PIN.
  • PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 200,000 iterations to derive the wrapping key from your PIN.
  • Master key never stored in cleartext. Wrapped in the iOS Keychain under kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly.
  • 10-attempt wipe. Ten consecutive wrong PINs permanently delete the master key. No recovery without your phrase.
  • Privacy overlay covers the UI in the App Switcher. What you have stays private.
  • Auto-lock zeroes the in-memory master key when the app backgrounds. Configurable delay.
# On-disk format for every encrypted blob
nonce[12] || ciphertext || tag[16]

# Master key derivation
salt ← SecRandomCopyBytes(16)
wrapping_key ← PBKDF2(PIN, salt, 200_000)
wrapped ← AES-GCM.seal(master_key, wrapping_key)

# Keychain storage
kSecAttrAccessible:
  WhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly
kSecAttrSynchronizable: false
